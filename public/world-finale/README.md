# World fashion finale artwork

The five transparent Duo costumes were generated for this presentation using `duo-winning-outfit.png` as the character and style reference, and encoded as high-quality WebP assets. They depict a Spanish traje de luces, an American baseball uniform, a Japanese kimono, Italian Renaissance dress and Chinese hanfu, in that order.

`world-land.svg` uses the public-domain Natural Earth 1:110m country outlines, projected to the finale's 1600 × 960 canvas. Source: [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/), [geometry](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson). The map and costumes are served locally; playback needs no external requests.

The six landmark illustrations are original SVG drawings in `src/WorldLandmarks.jsx`: the Eiffel Tower, Tokyo Tower, Gyeongbokgung Palace, the Colosseum, the Temple of Heaven and the Puerta de Alcalá.
